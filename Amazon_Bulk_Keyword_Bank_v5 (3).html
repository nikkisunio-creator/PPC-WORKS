How a keyword finds its ASIN

Every keyword and product target is joined to an advertised ASIN in this order, and the Mapped via column names the route taken for each row:

1  Product ad rows in the same ad group, matched on Campaign ID + Ad Group ID.

2  An ASIN written into the ad group name. This is what maps Sponsored Brands, whose sheets carry no product ads.

3  An ASIN written into the campaign name.

Effective status is the worst state in the campaign → ad group → target chain, so a live keyword in a paused campaign reads paused.
